# VibleNow — notes for App Review and Google Play review

Paste the "Reviewer notes" section into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes,
and into Play Console → App content / release notes for reviewers where asked.

## Reviewer notes (English)

VibleNow is a visual Bible reader. Each passage of the completed books is paired with an original,
reviewed illustration (books still in production show a placeholder until their art is ready) that changes as the reader scrolls through the text.
No account or sign-in is needed; open the app and start reading.

How to try the main features (about two minutes):
1. The app opens in John 1. Scroll the text — the illustration changes with each passage.
2. Tap the book name at the top to switch books (41 books), or search with the magnifier
   (try "John 3:16" or "love").
3. Use the flag menu to switch between Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese Scripture at the
   same verse.
4. Tap a verse to highlight it, write a private note, or share it. "Share image" creates a
   Scripture card and opens the system share sheet; "Save image" saves it to Photos.
5. The ⓘ button explains how the scene relates to the text; the speech-bubble button sends
   private feedback about an image.
6. Turn on Airplane Mode and reopen the app: all Scripture text, search and notes keep working.

Why this is an app and not only a website (Guideline 4.2):
- The complete Scripture text in four languages and the search index are bundled in the app
  and work fully offline; recently viewed illustrations are cached for offline reading.
- Native share sheet for verse links and image cards, saving cards to Photos (iOS) or files.
- vible.now verse links open directly at that passage in the app (Universal Links / App Links).
- iPad and Android tablet layouts with text beside the illustration; Android back navigation.
- Notes and highlights are stored only on the device.

Scripture texts and licenses (shown in the app under "Translation & sources"):
- Korean: 개역한글판 (Korean Revised Version, 1961), Korean Bible Society.
- English: Berean Standard Bible — public domain.
- Chinese: Open Chinese Contemporary Bible (Biblica, 2022) — CC BY-SA 4.0, with the required
  attribution on screen and on shared cards.
- Japanese: 口語訳 (1955) and 新改訳新約聖書 (1965) — copyright terms expired according to the
  Japan Bible Society.
Illustrations are original works created for this project.

User-generated content: the only user content is optional feedback about an image, which is sent
privately to the developer and never shown to other users. There is no social feed, chat or
public posting.

Privacy: no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. https://vible.now/privacy

## App Store Connect answers

- Category: Reference (secondary: Books)
- Age rating: no objectionable content categories selected except "Infrequent/Mild Realistic
  Violence" (Biblical scenes such as the crucifixion are depicted without gore). Expected 9+ / 12+.
- App Privacy: Data Not Collected for tracking. Data linked to the user: none.
  Feedback messages are "User Content → Other User Content", not linked to identity, used for
  App Functionality, optional. (If you prefer the simplest label, feedback can be described as
  "Customer Support".)
- Export compliance: uses only standard HTTPS — ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO (set in Info.plist).
- Availability: all countries and regions. Mainland China requires an ICP filing number and
  religious content is restricted there; expect that storefront to be rejected.

## Google Play answers

- App category: Books & Reference. Free. No ads.
- Data safety: no data shared with third parties. Data collected: "Other user-generated content"
  (feedback message) — optional, not linked to identity, used for app functionality, encrypted in
  transit, deletable on request by email. No location, contacts, photos, identifiers or analytics.
- Content rating (IARC): Reference/education app; mild realistic violence in Biblical art.
- Target audience: 13+ (not designed for children, but suitable for general audiences).
- Countries: all available countries.
